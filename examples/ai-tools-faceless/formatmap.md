## Premium upgrades for your easy formats

**Text on B-roll**
- [ ] *Polish:* Shoot your own footage that literally shows what the text says, not mood footage
- [ ] *Polish:* Set the text in one consistent typeface, size and safe-area position
- [ ] *Insight:* Replace the generic line with a number, a named example or a counter-intuitive claim
- [ ] *Insight:* Turn one line into a 3-beat mini argument across 3 shots
- [ ] *Novelty:* Make the text interact with the footage (behind objects, tracking, revealed by movement)
- [ ] *Novelty:* Use a recurring visual series hook the audience learns to recognise
- [ ] *Detail:* Time each line to be readable twice at normal speed
- [ ] *Detail:* Pick audio that fits the claim, not just what's trending

**Faceless explainer (stock + voiceover)**
- [ ] *Polish:* Replace generic stock with visuals that literally show each sentence
- [ ] *Polish:* Record or clone a voice with real pacing and warmth, and mix it with room tone and music
- [ ] *Insight:* Lead with a question the viewer actually has and answer it with one fresh source
- [ ] *Insight:* Add one original chart or comparison
- [ ] *Novelty:* Give the channel a signature visual style (collage, glass UI, sketch) instead of stock
- [ ] *Novelty:* Use a recurring visual metaphor across episodes
- [ ] *Detail:* Fact-check every number and show the source
- [ ] *Detail:* Remove watermarks and mismatched stock (wrong country, wrong era)
